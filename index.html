How to Play

Goal: Reach the finish line before others while dodging obstacles. If you crash, you restart at the beginning.

Controls (4 Rails):

◀ ▶ or [A / D]: Switch between the 4 fixed rail tracks.

⬆ or [W / Space]: Jump to dodge low obstacles.

+ / - or [E / Q]: Accelerate or decelerate your mine cart. (Speed holds constant unless keys are held down!)

Power-Ups (Pickaxes ❓):

Collide with a golden pickaxe to get an item advantage. Activate it with the yellow [USE] button or [S / Shift].

  • 🚀 Nitro: Temporary double speed boost.
  • 🛡️ Shield: Protects against 1 obstacle crash.
  • 👻 Ghost: Intangible through obstacles for 5 seconds.
  • 🦘 Super Jump: Your next jump is huge.
  • 🐢 Turtle: Drastically slows down all opponents.
  • 💎 Mini: Shrinks you to fit through tight gaps.
  • 💣 Bomb: Drops an explosive TNT block behind you.
  • ⚡ Lightning: Shocks and spins out all other players.
  • ❄️ Freeze: Encases other racers in block of ice.
  • 🧲 Magnet: Automatically pulls nearby items.
  • 🌀 Teleport: Blinks safely forward 45 meters.
  • 🔥 Fire Trail: Spawns flame trail that burns followers.
